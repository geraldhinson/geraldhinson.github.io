Engineers are a bit different kind of animal. (But, you knew that, right?)

We have spent thousands of man-years exploring user experiences, interfaces, and human factors in an attempt to make software more useful and simpler - and if you're thinking, "with questionable success!" you're not necessarily wrong.

But, given all of that investment, what could be more fun than a contest to see who could build things the most wrong? With the only rule being that your creation had to actually work.

And with that bit of preamble, I present to you this hilarious misuse of great engineering talent - which I 100% support and only regret not being a participant!
